The
Lift

Five prompts. Name anything that fits. The more niche your answer, the higher the lift climbs: past three New York towers, up to the Empire State Building, or straight out into space.

How it works. You get 25 seconds per prompt. A wrong answer flashes FLOOR NOT RECOGNISED and you can keep guessing until the time runs out; if it does, the floor isn't chosen and you move on. Every possible answer has been sorted into five levels of nicheness. Common answers only reach the first tower, and the deepest cuts go interstellar. If you misspell something, the lift asks if you meant the closest answer; press Enter again to lock it in.
